## Cheat sheet

| Segment | Formula |
|---|---|
| 01 Imposter syndrome | I'm not **[X]**, but I am **[Y]**, and the room needs that because **[Z]**. |
| 02 Your talk | This talk is about **[topic]** for **[audience]** so that when they leave, they can **[verb + takeaway]**. "Understand" doesn't count. |
| 03 Story arc | Pick a frame. Box 1 names the stakes. Four tension points, each higher than the last, then the climax slide, then the takeaway. |
| 04 Opening | **[Hook]** + **[Who you are, one sentence]** + **[What the room gets, one sentence]**. End on a line, then say thank you. |
| 05 Nerves | Fist clench → box breathing 4·4·4·4 → anchor phrase. Blank mind: pause, breathe, slide title, key point. |
| 06 Voice and body | Pitch drop, whisper, 3-second pause. Walk on transitions, plant on points. Three eye-contact zones. |
| 07 Hot seat | One thing that worked. One thing to try. |

## What's next

- **This week:** write the three boxes out as slide titles, one per slide. If a slide doesn't have a title that says something, cut it.
- **Before you submit a CFP:** use your Segment 02 sentence as the last line of your abstract.
- **Before the talk:** run the full opening and closing out loud, standing, three times. Run the middle once.
- **After the talk:** check your commitment card. Did you do it?
