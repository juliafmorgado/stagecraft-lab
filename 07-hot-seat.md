# Hot seat

You've written it. Now say it to the room. Saying it out loud once to real people is worth ten silent read-throughs.

Nobody gets picked. You choose your rung.

## Pick your rung

1. **Read your hook.** Seated, 10 seconds.
2. **The full 60 seconds (Seated).** Hook, who you are, what the room gets.
3. **The full 60 seconds (On stage).** Walk up, plant your feet, look across the room.
{: .steps}

Any rung counts. Reading your hook from your seat is a complete share. Most people who start on rung 1 are surprised by how quickly they want rung 3.

## How it runs · 11 min

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 min | **Set up:** Hosts explain the rungs and the feedback rule. |
| 3 min | **Hook round:** Anyone on rung 1 calls out their hook from their seat. Rapid fire, no feedback, just applause. |
| 6 min | **Hot seat:** Three volunteers step up for rung 2 or 3. Each gets 60 seconds of speaking followed by 1 minute of focused feedback. |
| 1 min | **Wrap up:** Hosts highlight one key pattern of improvement. |

## Feedback rule

Listeners give exactly two pieces of feedback—nothing more, nothing less:

> **1. What worked:** "When you [action], I [reaction]."
>
> **2. What to try:** "Next time, try [suggestion]."
{: .card data-label="Feedback card"}

Keep it specific and focused on observable actions, not personality. *"When you paused before the outage stat, I leaned in"* is actionable. *"You're a natural"* is not.

## Scorecard for listeners

Use this scorecard while watching. It's built to sharpen your own eye, not to grade the speaker.

| Target | What to listen & look for |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Did the opening line jump straight into the story or problem? |
| **Who they are** | Was there a single line establishing why *they* are sharing this? |
| **The takeaway** | Did you hear an actionable verb describing what the room gets? |
| **Pause** | Was there at least one deliberate pause for emphasis? |
| **Stance** | Did they plant their feet firmly on the key line? |
| **Eye contact** | Did they engage left, center, and right sections of the room? |
| **Ending** | Did they land on a strong final sentence—and avoid ending on *"So, yeah..."*? |
