# Stagecraft Lab

A 60-minute speaking and storytelling lab for engineers, builders, and anyone looking to give a public talk.

By the end of this lab, you'll walk away with a reframed mindset, a clear story arc, and a 60-second opening for your next talk.

## The ladder

We'll go through eight short segments that build on each other and end with a single line spoken aloud.

{% include ladder.html %}

## How this works

- **Keep this page open on your phone.** Every segment has fill-in boxes. What you type is saved locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the hosts can't see it.
- **Everyone participates without feeling put on the spot.** Every segment concludes with a one-line share: hosts take three volunteers before the whole room speaks theirs aloud together.
- **Rough is fine.** You're building a first draft in an hour. You'll polish it later.

## Pick your topic now

This whole lab centers on one talk. Pick yours before starting Segment 02. Don't worry if it doesn't exist yet or feels small!

Ask yourself:

- What are you working on right now?
- What did you learn the hard way?
- What have you explained to a colleague three times?
- What broke, and what did you do about it?

**Already have an accepted CFP?** Use it. Paste the abstract into the box below and work on that talk all the way through.
